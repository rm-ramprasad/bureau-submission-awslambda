How to submit to the credit bureaus

Six steps from your loan data to the bureau files. Consumer and Commercial follow the same steps; each is checked against its own bureau format.

The flow at a glance

  1. 1New request Choose the as-of date, the reporting cycle and Consumer and/or Commercial. You · 1 minute
  2. 2Exceptions Leave out a loan or customer, or correct a value, with a reason. Optional. You · as needed
  3. 3Validate Submit the request. The system reads the LMS and checks every record against the bureau rules. System · a few minutes
  4. 4Review findings See what will be reported, what will not, and why. You · before generating
  5. 5Generate files Pick the bureaus. One file per bureau, made from the validated data. You + system · a minute
  6. 6Upload to the bureaus Download each file and upload it to that bureau as agreed with them. You · outside this app

↺ Something to fix after step 4? Correct it in the LMS or add an exception, then raise a new request. A validated request is never edited, so every file always matches what was checked.

Each step in detail

1 New request

  • As-of date: the date the data is reported for, usually the last day of the month.
  • Reporting cycle: ME (month end), W1, W2 or W3 (weekly), or ad hoc.
  • Types: Consumer, Commercial or both. Each is validated separately.

The request starts as a draft, so you can add exceptions before anything runs.

2 Exceptions (optional)

  • Exclude a loan (by loan number) or a customer (all their loans).
  • Override days past due, balance, amount overdue or settlement amount.
  • Every exception needs a reason; they are kept for audit.

Exceptions are locked once the request is submitted for validation.

3 Validate

  • Click Submit for validation on the request.
  • The system copies the data from the LMS and runs the bureau checks. The request shows In progress until it is done.

4 Review findings

  • Open the request: four numbers show records checked, will be reported, not reported and reported with gaps.
  • Open a category to see its issues, then View for the loans or borrowers concerned.
  • Download CSV gives every finding, to share with the operations team.

5 Generate files

  • On the validated request, choose the bureaus and click Generate files.
  • Records that were not reported are left out; the exceptions file lists them.
  • Files are never overwritten: each generation is a new file, kept for audit.

6 Upload to the bureaus

  • Download each file from the request or from Files.
  • Upload it to the bureau it was made for (the bureau's name is in the file name and on the Files page).

This step happens outside this app.

Where is my request?

The dashboard counts requests by stage. What each stage means and what to do next:

Awaiting exceptions Draft. Add exceptions if needed, then submit it for validation.
In progress Validation or file generation is running. Wait a few minutes.
Validated, files pending Review the findings, then generate the files.
Completed Files are ready. Download them and upload them to the bureaus.
Failed Open the request and read the error. Retry it, or raise a new request.
Cancelled Discarded or cancelled. Nothing more to do.

Reading the findings

Every record gets one of these results. A record is one loan and one borrower (Consumer: account × holder; Commercial: credit facility × borrower).

Not reportedThe record breaks a rule the bureau requires, so it is left out of the file.Fix it in the LMS, or exclude it with a reason.
Reported with gapsThe record is reported, but a value or a section (for example an invalid phone number) is left out.Fix it in the LMS when you can.
Warning onlyReported as it is. Worth a look, nothing is left out.No action needed for this submission.

Words used in the app

Request
One validation of your data for an as-of date, and the files generated from it.
As-of date
The date the reported data refers to (balances, days past due ...).
Reporting cycle
ME month end, W1 / W2 / W3 weekly submissions, or ad hoc.
Exception
Your instruction for one request: leave a loan or customer out, or correct a value.
Validation
The check of every record against the bureau rules, before any file is made.
Finding
One issue found in one record, with its effect on the file.
Bureau file
The file for one bureau (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF High Mark), in its required format.
Consumer / Commercial
Loans to individuals / credit facilities of businesses: separate files and rules.

For administrators
  • Rules & policies lists every check. An administrator can switch a check off or change its effect (for example from "Not reported" to "Warning only") for this NBFC, with a reason.
  • Rule parameters (dates, minimum lengths, ID patterns) can be changed on the same page.
  • Custom checks specific to this NBFC can be added there and previewed on the latest validation.
  • Every change is recorded in Audit and applies to requests validated afterwards.
  • A request that keeps failing with a technical error: contact your support team with the request number.
